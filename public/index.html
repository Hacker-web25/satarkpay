The money waits. The AI checks. A person decides.

Built on a payment pattern most people never see: money can be authorized now and captured later. Until it's captured, it doesn't move.

  1. 1

    Hold

    The payment is created with intent to authorize. The money is reserved, not sent.

    PayPal Orders v2 · AUTHORIZE
  2. 2

    Check

    An AI model reads the payee, amount, note and payment history against known scam patterns, then scores and explains the risk.

    Risk model + LLM explanation
  3. 3

    Ask

    Everyday payments go straight through. Risky ones go to a trusted family member for a one-tap decision.

    Trusted circle
  4. 4

    Release or cancel

    Approved: the payment is captured. Blocked: the authorization is voided and nothing ever left.

    Capture · Void · Webhooks

Different scripts. The same fingerprints.

Every scam needs urgency, secrecy, or a payment that can't come back. सतर्कPay looks for those fingerprints, so it catches scams nobody has reported yet.
